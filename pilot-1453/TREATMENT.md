# Pilot 1453 — "The Last Spring" (TREATMENT)

Purpose: a ~40 s test of the channel's locked look and voice, not a published video. Styles: `engraving` (main) + `archive-lighttable` (evidence moment), joined by a loupe. Voice: Kokoro `bm_george`, en-gb, speed 0.78. Format 1920×1080.

## Three candidate structures
1. **States of one plate** — one engraved plan of the city; the plate is re-cut/annotated as each sentence lands (walls → defenders → enemy), then the loupe drops onto a real archive object, then colour blooms over the city on the last line.
2. **Book of plates** — three small plates turned like pages, one fact each.
3. **The proof** — open on mirrored copper, peel the sheet to reveal the true print, loupe in.

**Chosen: 1.** It shows the channel's two modes (cut line → real object) in one continuous sheet, and the loupe handoff is the transition grammar every episode will reuse.

## Logline / arc
A city that has stood for eleven hundred years is cut line by line, then examined under a loupe as evidence, then touched by colour — the moment it is about to fall.
Setup (the plate begins) → turn (the loupe lands on the real record) → ending (the wash blooms; the plate is signed).

## Benchmark (grammar only)
Learn: 19th-century atlas plates (title in roman caps over an italic line, plate mark, roundels with leaders); archive documentaries that push into one document with one reading per screen. Don't take: any specific plate, border, title or layout.

## Beat sheet / cue map
Narration drives it (word timings from the ASR check). Music: solo viola da gamba or plucked lute, sparse, ~half under speech; two silences (before shot 4 and after the last word).

## Sound design
- Room: quiet workroom, faint clock. Foley: burin hiss + tick on lift (1–3), paper slip tap, loupe glass set-down + tiny ring (4), wet brush + plip (5). Music: plucked, dry while lines are cut; one sustained bow note on the wash.

## Type
